## Brand & Style

This design system expresses pure, wholesome farm freshness translated into an elevated, modern digital commerce and management platform. It synthesizes organic warmth with clinical agricultural clarity—bridging daily consumer e-commerce (raw milk, artisanal butter, fresh yogurts) and administrative farm operations (yield monitoring, livestock batches, subscription fulfillment).

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Tactile Modern Organic**: Natural warmth anchored by structured layouts, airy whitespace, and crisp precision. The atmosphere avoids rustic grit in favor of clean, hygienic agricultural excellence.
- **Bilingual Balance**: Designed natively for symmetrical harmony between English (LTR) and Urdu/Arabic (RTL) scripts, respecting horizontal flow, line heights, and typography balance.
- **Trust & Purity**: Conveys freshness, halal integrity, and farm-to-table traceability through luminous, unpolluted backgrounds, leaf-inspired accents, and warm golden touches.

## Colors

The palette centers on wholesome agricultural freshness, sunlight, and raw dairy purity.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#218838` - Fresh Green)**: Key interactive states, primary action triggers, purchase confirmations, and active health indicators.
- **Secondary (`#14532D` - Deep Forest Green)**: Institutional authority, top-level headings, header backgrounds, navigation chrome, and grounding surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#F4C542` - Golden Butter)**: Promotional tags, dairy fat ratings, subscription perks, star ratings, and celebratory alerts.
- **Neutral (`#263238` - Dark Charcoal)**: High-legibility editorial text, data readouts, and core iconography.
- **Canvas (`#FFFDF5` - Soft Cream)**: The primary ambient canvas, preventing clinical glare and evoking raw, rich dairy milk.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp White)**: Elevated cards, sheet panels, product tiles, and modals.
- **Subtle Tint (`#F3F7F1` - Light Mint)**: Hover states, badge backgrounds, table striping, and soft notification backdrops.
- **Outline (`#E2E8F0` - Soft Grey / Light Sage)**: Low-friction separation lines, metric borders, and form boundaries.

## Typography

The type system balances geometric clarity with bilingual elegance. **Outfit** serves as the primary Latin family, featuring open counters and friendly circular geometry that harmonize with clean dairy packaging. 

### Bilingual Support (Urdu / Arabic)
- For Urdu strings (`اردو`), fall back gracefully to **Noto Nastaliq Urdu** or **Noto Sans Arabic** with a line-height multiplier of `1.35x` to prevent clipping of extended vertical diacritics and ligatures.
- Maintain consistent baseline alignment when English metrics (e.g., "1 Liter / ۱ لیٹر") are presented side by side.
- Numbers in transactional tables and pricing badges should default to tabular lining (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing

A structured, responsive grid system underpins both consumer catalog browsing and analytical farm management.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥1200px)**: 12-column layout, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `2rem` (32px) exterior margins. Content max-width is constrained to `1280px` for optimal reading and scanning.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, and `1.5rem` (24px) outer margin.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout, `1rem` (16px) gutters, and `1rem` (16px) margins.

### Rhythm & Alignment
Spacing follows an organic 4px/8px modular rhythm. Component stacks prefer `space-md` (`1rem`) for dense administrative views and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) for consumer-facing storefront displays.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines warm ambient light with low-contrast borders. Surfaces maintain a clean, physical presence without heavy drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Background canvas (`#FFFDF5`). No elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Shelf Surfaces)**: Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and an ambient, warm green-tinted shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Panels)**: Elevated surface with `0 10px 20px -4px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)` and border transitioning to `#218838/20`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-overs, & Cart Drawers)**: Deep focus elevation: `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.14), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The shape system leverages level `2` rounded geometry, reflecting the smooth, fluid nature of milk and dairy products while maintaining commercial rigor.

- **Base Radius (`rounded-md`, 0.5rem / 8px)**: Inputs, buttons, segmented selectors, and operational table containers.
- **Card Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px)**: Product cards, inventory stat boxes, delivery calendars, and modal panels.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full`, 9999px)**: Status badges, category chips, unit switchers, and quantity steppers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#218838` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius. On hover: shifts to `#14532D` with subtle lift.
- **Secondary / Accent Action**: Solid `#F4C542` background, `#263238` bold text. Used selectively for checkout actions, subscription renewals, or primary promotional CTAs.
- **Outline Action**: White background, 1.5px border in `#218838`, `#218838` text. Fills with `#F3F7F1` on hover.

### Product Cards
- Encapsulated in `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border and `1rem` radius.
- Includes a top visual frame for fresh batch photography, followed by dairy purity metrics (e.g., Fat % / FAT-SNF ratio pill badges in `#F3F7F1`).
- Contains an inline **Unit Selector** (e.g., `0.5L`, `1L`, `5L`, or `1kg`, `5kg`) with a segmented pill control.
- Features a dual-state **Add to Cart** CTA: a single-tap button that morphs into a numeric stepper (`-`, `qty`, `+`) upon item addition.

### Badges & Status Pills
- Compact, pill-shaped tags with `11px` bold typography.
- **Fresh Batch**: `#F3F7F1` background, `#14532D` text, dot indicator in `#218838`.
- **Pre-Order / Low Stock**: Light amber tint background, `#B45309` text, `#F4C542` dot indicator.
- **Certified Organic / Pure**: Solid `#14532D` background, `#FFFFFF` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Standard height `44px` with a subtle `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, and text in `#263238`.
- Focus state activates a 1.5px border in `#218838` accompanied by a soft glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(33, 136, 56, 0.15)`.

### Operational Data Tables
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `#F3F7F1` header row and uppercase `11px` tracking labels in `#14532D`.
- Alternating subtle row separator lines in `#E2E8F0`. Hover row state highlighted via `#FFFDF5`.
- Dedicated numeric cells for daily liter yield, milking batches, and temperature logs set with tabular numerical alignment.
